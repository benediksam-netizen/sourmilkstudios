Player guide

How to play Dino Rampage

Dino Rampage: Eat & Grow! is a free 3D dinosaur game that runs in your web browser on computers, tablets and phones. You start as a young dinosaur in the Canyon Oasis, eat to earn XP, level up, and open new parts of the island one by one. This guide covers the controls, every dinosaur, the areas and how to grow fast.

Choose your dinosaur

There are 15 animals to play, and each one has its own speed, jump and strength. Meat eaters attack other animals for food. Plant eaters graze and hold Defend to block an attack and hit back. Sea reptiles swim fast, and flying reptiles take to the air. In the game each one also has a short fact card, so you learn a little palaeontology while you play. For example, a Plesiosaurus is a sea reptile, not a dinosaur.

GroupAnimalsGood to know

The island and its areas

You begin in the Canyon Oasis. Each level you reach opens a new area with its own animals to eat:

  • Grassland (level 1): rabbits are quick, bison are big.
  • Deep Forest (level 2): boars and deer hide between the trees.
  • Sunny Sea (level 3): the home of the sea reptiles.
  • Winter Peaks (level 4): penguins waddle and yaks roam the snow.
  • Volcano (level 5): the toughest area. Watch out, the lava pools burn!

Grow faster

  • Look for rare food. Food comes as Common, Uncommon, Rare, Special and Event. Rare, Special and Event food also give bonus coins, but they take longer to come back once eaten.
  • Head for new areas. Each area you open has new animals to eat, so move on once you level up.
  • Finish tasks. Tasks give coins, and there is a hidden coin tucked away in every area.
  • Buy upgrades in the shop. Growth Boost gives more XP from everything, and Swift Feet makes you walk, run and swim faster.
  • Open the Daily Free Box. One free spin every day for coins and a small chance of a box-only skin.

Skins and outfits

Every dinosaur can wear skins. Some unlock as you level up (Sunset at level 2, then Midnight, Golden, Candy and Frost), others cost coins in the shop (Crystal, Neon, Shadow and Royal). You can also dress your dino with a hat and a body item, and friends see your outfit in the world.

Game mode: Dinosaur Hide & Seek

On the farm map, one player is the dinosaur and everyone else hides as a farm animal. Hiders should walk slowly, because running looks suspicious. Hold Graze (G on a keyboard) to blend in, and use the noise, dance and laugh emotes (keys 1, 2 and 3) to taunt the hunter for bonus coins. The dino presses Sniff (K on a keyboard) to show a circle where a hider is close by, then has to work out which animal is a player. Play straight away against bots, or pick Real players and invite your friends.

How to play Ocean Bound

Ocean Bound is a free 3D fishing and island adventure game that runs in your web browser. You start in your own home on the Starting Island, get your first rod, then take a boat out to discover new seas, fill your Fishdex and face legendary bosses. This guide covers the controls, how fishing works, where to sail and how to level up.

Stuck in a hole? Press the round-arrow button at the top right to respawn. The corner-arrows button plays the game in full screen.

How the fishing fight works

When a fish bites, a needle appears on the reel gauge. Reel while the needle sits in the green. Push it too far right and the line snaps; leave it slack too long and the fish slips off. Surging fish pull harder, so let go of F until they settle and then reel again. Heavy fish load your reel and tire you out faster, which is why better gear matters later on. Once you have landed a reef fish in the Coral Shallows, Old Marlo's shipment of rods, reels, lines and hooks arrives at the Fishing Shop.

Where to fish

Every area has its own fish, and the Fishdex keeps a page for each one. Catch a species once to unlock its entry. The waters to explore are:

  • Starting Island and the open sea: your home, the village shops and your first catches.
  • Coral Shallows and Sunny Shores: bright reef fish close to home.
  • Dino Island, south of the Starting Island: meet Kai in the beach village, explore the jungle and highlands, dig for fossils in Fossil Valley with your Explorer's Brush, open the Forgotten Temple and walk the boardwalks of Mangrove Bay. Donate one of every fossil to the Island Museum to hear the island's story.
  • The Snowy Seas, far to the north-west: tie up at Snow Village, climb the Frozen Peaks, sail to Glacier Bay, the Ice Arch, the Hot Springs and the Iceberg Fields, help Bjorn's workshop, and follow the quests down four cave levels to the Frost Leviathan.
  • The Blossom Islands, far to the west: nine places around Blossom Village, from the torii trail of Moonview Peak to Petal Falls, Bamboo Isle, the Hidden Lagoon, the Coral Atoll and four levels of the Spirit Grotto. On some afternoons a Petal Wind blows and special fish rise.
  • Volcano Isles, the Ancient Ruins and the Mystic Deep: farther waters with rarer fish for bigger boats.

Pearls and levels

Every catch, cooked dish, quest and discovery earns angler XP. Each level gives you a pearl and makes rare fish a little more likely to bite. Pearls also come from treasure chests floating at sea, rare fish and achievements. Spend them on outfits, boat paint and garden decorations. Sell your catch to Nana Pia for coins, and claim a coin reward from Professor Nami each time your Fishdex reaches the next milestone.

Legends and bosses

Every biome hides a boss. Look for a dark whirlpool near each island (it appears on your map once you have found the island), buy a Legend Lure from Nana Pia and cast right into it. Bosses fight in three phases and need a big boat and heavy gear. A beaten boss returns the next day, and each win goes in your Trophy Room.

In the game

A world that runs on real cargo

Every ship on the map is a real shipment from one company to another. Take freight contracts, buy goods where they are cheap and sell them where they are scarce, and watch your own ships live as they sail. Storms and busy ports can slow everything down, so keep your fleet in good repair.

Two ways to play

Normal mode or Pro mode

Normal mode keeps the focus on shipping, trading and building your own factories. Pro mode adds the full stock and bond market, central banks and company takeovers. A guided tutorial with our milk-carton mascot starts with every new game.

Player guide

Getting started in Sour Milk Logistics

You run a small shipping company inside a working world economy of ten countries and 79 companies. Here is what to pick on the title screen, how the clock works and what each screen is for. The full guide (PDF above) goes through every tutorial step and the finance behind it.

Three choices before a new game

ChoiceOptionsWhat to pick
ModeNormal or ProNormal first. Pro adds the full stock and bond market, central banks, interest rates, inflation and economic news.
Starting capitalFounder $12.48M · Investor $1B · Tycoon $60BFounder is the intended game. Tycoon can buy control of any company on day one.
Guided tutorialPlay it or skip itPlay it. It takes about two minutes.

The clock

The game starts paused. Press Play at the top, or the space bar, to start and pause. One day lasts 12 seconds at Play speed, and you can change this to 30, 20, 12, 6 or 2 seconds in the menu. A year has 360 days, a quarter closes every 90 days, and the game saves itself every 30 days in your browser.

Your first moves

  • Put your ships to work. Your two feeder ships cost about $11,000 a day even when they sit in port, so cash only goes down until they carry something.
  • Take a freight contract from the Contracts screen for steady income while you learn the map.
  • Trade the price gaps. On the Trade screen, buy cargo in a port where it is cheap and sell it where it is scarce.
  • Keep the fleet repaired. Storms and busy ports slow ships down, and worn ships cost you time.
  • Look inside companies. Every ship on the map is a real shipment between two companies. Open one to see its factories, suppliers and finances.

The ten screens

World shows the live map and your objectives. Companies lets you look inside any company. Market has commodity prices and stocks, plus bonds and central banks in Pro mode. Trade is for buying and selling cargo between ports. Contracts lists freight offers from shippers. Factories is where you found your own producer. Fleet holds your ships, repairs and the shipyard. Research has six projects that improve your fleet, contracts, trading and building. Finance shows net worth, cash, your credit line and shareholdings. News reports what happens in the simulation.

SHOP. COOK. SERVE. DECORATE.

Run your own little restaurant in first person. Shop for fresh ingredients around a sunny seaside town, cook real recipes from six cuisines and serve every guest a meal they love. Play solo, or cook together with up to three friends.

SERVEHappy guests, good reviews

In the game

Shop where the food comes from

Every morning, walk through town with your crate. Buy meat at the butcher, fish at the docks, cheese and eggs at the dairy, bread at the bakery and fresh vegetables at the market. Carry it all back and fill up your fridge before the doors open.

In the kitchen

Cook every dish step by step

Chop on the board, fry and stir in the pan, boil in the pot and bake in the oven. Watch the heat dial so nothing burns. Pick an Italian, Chinese, Indonesian, Indian, Mediterranean or Japanese menu, and the recipe cards tell you every step and where to buy each ingredient.

Run the restaurant

Serve your guests and grow

Take orders at the table, bring each plate while it is hot and collect the money when guests leave. Good meals earn stars and experience. Level up, buy better equipment and decorate the dining room to make it cozy.

Expand

Open a second restaurant

Once your first restaurant is doing well, buy the empty shop by the harbour. Give it a name and its own cuisine, build the kitchen piece by piece, set the menu and prices, and hire a manager to run it while you cook.

Play together

Cook with friends, keep your progress

Type your chef name and a 4-number PIN and your restaurant is saved online, so you can carry on from any device. Make a friend server with a 4-letter code and up to four chefs can share one kitchen. The server stays saved, so everyone can leave and come back later.
